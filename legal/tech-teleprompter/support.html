Tech Teleprompter Support

For iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Email Contact@hugglerholdings.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the issue. A short description is enough to start.

Record your first take

  1. Open the sample script, or tap Add Script to write, paste, or import plain text.
  2. Tap Record in the script editor. If prompted, allow the camera and microphone. Choose a recording style or tap Not Now.
  3. Read from the scrolling panel while the camera records. Tap Stop when you finish.
  4. Play the take in review, save a copy to Photos, or share it. You can find it later under Takes.

Camera or microphone access

Open Settings and find Tech Teleprompter. On newer iOS versions, go to Settings, then Apps, then Tech Teleprompter. Turn on Camera and Microphone for recording. The script library and editor are available without these permissions.

Wait When I Pause

On iOS 26 or later, turn this on in the studio's Teleprompter settings. When the sound meter is available, say a line and then pause for about two seconds during a take. The script waits for you and resumes when you speak again. The camera keeps recording.

Save a video or still frame

Open a recorded take and choose Save to Photos. To save a still, pause on the frame and choose Save Frame. Allow add-only Photos access if prompted. Saving to Photos creates a separate copy.

Manage storage

Save or share any take you want to keep outside the app before deleting it. Delete takes from Takes or the review screen to free space. Deleting an app recording leaves any separate copies in Photos or other destinations.
