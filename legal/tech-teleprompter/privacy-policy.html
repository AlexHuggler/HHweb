Tech Teleprompter Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Tech Teleprompter iPhone app from Huggler Holdings LLC.

Tech Teleprompter stores your scripts, settings, and recordings on your iPhone. Huggler Holdings LLC does not collect data through the app. The app has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, or tracking.

Camera, microphone, and motion

The recording studio uses the camera and microphone to show your camera preview and record video with sound. The sound meter, Sound Check, and the optional Wait When I Pause setting use microphone levels on your iPhone. The optional level reads device motion to show tilt. The app uses these sound and motion measurements on your iPhone and does not save them.

Files, Photos, and sharing

The app reads the plain-text file you choose to import or the text you choose to paste. It asks for add-only Photos access when you choose to save a video or still frame. It does not read your photo library. If you choose to export or share content, your selected destination handles its copy under its own privacy practices.

Storage, backups, and deletion

Scripts and settings are stored in the app and may be included in Apple device backups. Recordings and their details, including notes, are excluded from those backups. The app also checks available space and reads metadata for its own files to manage recordings and show those details on your iPhone.

You can delete individual scripts and takes in the app. Deleting a script keeps its recordings. Deleting the app removes its local data. Manage any separate copies in Photos, other destinations, or device backups where you saved them.

You can change camera, microphone, Photos, and any available motion permissions in iPhone Settings.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and investigate your request. We keep correspondence as needed for those purposes and to meet legal obligations. You can request deletion or ask a privacy question at Contact@hugglerholdings.com.
